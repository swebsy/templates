# Afterparty — free events website template

![Afterparty](thumb.webp)

A one-page party invitation that opens as you scroll: a card that says "We eloped." swings open to "You're invited.", then the night in order, the venue, what to know and an RSVP.

Good for: Wedding and elopement parties, Launch and opening nights, Birthdays and anniversaries, Any one-night invitation with an RSVP.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/afterparty/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=afterparty&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/afterparty/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
