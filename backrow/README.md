# Backrow — free local business website template

![Backrow](thumb.webp)

A yoga and Pilates studio site for nervous beginners: regulars by where they put their mat, the week, three ways to start and prices.

Good for: Small yoga and Pilates studios, Studios that book through Momence or Mindbody, Teachers running beginners' courses, Neighbourhood movement and barre studios.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/backrow/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=backrow&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/backrow/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
