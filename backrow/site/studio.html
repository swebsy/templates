Find us

Twelve Mats opened in 2019 in an empty room above a print shop: tall windows, a wooden floor, and space for exactly twelve mats with room to stretch.

A few of the earliest regulars painted the wall that first spring. We kept every class at twelve so the teacher can see everyone, and nobody has to squeeze in.

Two teachers, a week of small classes, and a lot of people who come back.

Mara and Sami.

The two teachers take each other’s classes every week. You’ll find them at the back.
