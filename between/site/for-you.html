For you

You don’t have to want to be here yet.

Lots of people come because someone else thought they should. That’s a fine place to start. After three sessions we look at it together, and you get a real say.

Book a free call ↓

What a session is like.

Where you sit.

There’s the sofa or the armchair across from mine. Pick either. Move halfway through if you want.

Your phone.

Keep it. Sometimes it’s easier to show me a message than to explain it.

Quiet is fine.

Nobody has to fill the silence. Some sessions are slow. That still counts.

What we talk about.

You pick. If you don’t know where to start, I’ll ask about your week, not your childhood.

When you say no.

You say “pass.”

We move on. I don’t come back to it unless you do.

You don’t say anything.

That’s allowed. Sometimes I talk for a while, or we play cards. People talk more with their hands busy.

You think I’m wrong.

Tell me. I’d rather know, and sometimes you’ll be right.

“Will you tell my parents?”

No

Not what you say here. The details are yours.

Some things, and you’ll know

If they started this with you: whether you’re coming, how it’s going in general, the goals you agreed.

Only if I have to

Serious risk to you or someone else, suspected abuse, or a court order. When it’s safe and possible, I’ll talk with you first.
