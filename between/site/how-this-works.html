Who hears what, in full.

Stays private

What you say here.

The details are yours. That includes what you tell me about friends, school, family, sex, drugs, and anything you’d rather your parents didn’t know.

When it’s safe and possible, I’ll talk with you before I share anything.

Your parent hears, and you know when

If a parent started this with you.

  • Whether you’re coming.
  • How it’s going, in general.
  • The goals you agreed together.

Before each parent call, I tell you what I plan to say.

When I may have to share without asking

For everyone, at any age.

  • If you or someone else is at serious risk.
  • Suspected abuse, which I report to the authorities.
  • A court order.

Maryland lets a therapist share some information with a parent, even when a teen started on their own. My practice is to share only what’s on this page.

Started on your own, or 18 or over? Your parents hear only what you sign for. The safety and legal exceptions still apply to everyone.
