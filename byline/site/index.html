Lakeshore Ledger · March 2026 · Investigation

One courtroom, 40 evictions a morning

I sat in Room 400 for 61 mornings and logged every case. Nine in ten tenants had no lawyer, and most hearings were over in three minutes.

What happened next

The county funded a right-to-counsel pilot for 1,200 families and cited the series in its budget vote.

The landlord with 412 addresses and no phone number

Cream City Public Radio · June 2025 · Audio series

Waiting for Section 8

The voucher waitlist opened for 72 hours. Eleven thousand families applied for 3,000 places, and I followed four of them for a year.

What happened next

The housing authority added a text-message status line after listeners said they couldn't check their place.

About

I grew up in a rented duplex on Milwaukee's south side, studied journalism at Marquette University and joined the Lakeshore Ledger, a nonprofit newsroom, the week after I graduated in 2023.

I read court filings and property records most days, file a lot of open-records requests, and interview in English and Spanish. My stories are built to be checked: every number links to its source.

What I bring

Records

Open-records requests, court dockets, property and tax data.

Data

Spreadsheets and SQL to build my own datasets from documents.

Interviews

In English and Spanish, with tenants, landlords and officials.

Audio

Reported, scripted and voiced a three-part radio series.
