# Cadenza — free local business website template

![Cadenza](thumb.webp)

A community music school website: lessons by age, one rate for every instrument, teachers who still perform, and the next student recital set as a printed concert program.

Good for: Music schools and academies, Piano, guitar and voice teachers, Lesson studios and tutors, Community arts schools.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/cadenza/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=cadenza&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/cadenza/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
