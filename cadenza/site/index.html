Starting takes one afternoon

Step 1

Book a free trial

Thirty minutes, any instrument, or tell us you’re not sure. We reply within one school day with two or three times.

Step 2

Meet your teacher

Try the instrument with them. We have every one here, in every size, and nobody buys anything before the second month.

Step 3

Pick a weekly slot

Pay by the 12‑week term. Pause for a term whenever life gets busy, and we hold your slot.
