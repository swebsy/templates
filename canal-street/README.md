# Canal Street — free nonprofit website template

![Canal Street](thumb.webp)

A volunteer pickup basketball club drawn on its own court lines: a centre-circle hero, the week as a lane, levels on the three-point arc.

Good for: Pickup and rec sports clubs, Community sports groups, Running and social clubs.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/canal-street/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=canal-street&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/canal-street/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
