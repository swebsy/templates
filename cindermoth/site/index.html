LAUNCH TRAILER

Light the Way

The lantern is not a lamp, it is the level design. Ledges, handholds and doorways only exist where the light falls, and the flame burns down as you walk. Every route is a decision about how much dark you can afford.

Draw Your Own Map

Nothing is filled in for you. The map opens blank and stays that way until you ink it yourself, room by room, in whatever shorthand you invent. Your marks are the only record the game keeps.

Walk, Don’t Fight

There is no combat and no death screen. The pressure comes from weather that closes roads on its own clock, from fuel you have to ration, and from the distance still between you and home.
