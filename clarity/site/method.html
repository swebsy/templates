The Northline method

A method for decisions that hold.

We turn incomplete evidence and competing views into a direction your team can explain, choose, and act on.

Designed for

High-stakes choices

Built around

Evidence and trade-offs

Measured by

Movement, not volume

The sequence

Four moves. One clear direction.

Each move earns the next. We keep the work visible so the team can challenge the logic, build conviction, and carry it forward.

01

Frame

Define the decision, the stakes, and the constraints worth respecting.

02

Find

Locate the few signals that change how the choice should be understood.

03

Focus

Make the trade-offs explicit and commit to the advantage that matters.

04

Move

Translate direction into a sequence of decisions, owners, and proof points.

How the work runs

Enough rigor. No theatre.

The process expands where uncertainty is real and compresses where the answer is already clear.

FRAME

Start with the choice

Leadership interviews, decision framing, constraints, success measures, and a shared working question.

FIND

Build the evidence base

Market signals, customer evidence, commercial patterns, competitor choices, and the assumptions most worth testing.

FOCUS

Choose where to play

Options, implications, decision workshops, a clear position, and a short list of deliberate non-priorities.

MOVE

Put direction to work

A sequenced roadmap, named owners, near-term moves, learning loops, and a story the wider team can repeat.
