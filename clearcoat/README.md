# Clearcoat — free local business website template

![Clearcoat](thumb.webp)

A paint-care and detailing studio: a before/after slider, treatments with honest limits and prices, and job notes on what was agreed.

Good for: Car detailers, Paint correction and ceramic coating studios, Mobile valeting.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/clearcoat/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=clearcoat&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/clearcoat/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
