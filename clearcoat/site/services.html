Before you ask

The five things owners ask most. Anything else, put it in your request and we'll answer within a working day.

What if the test spot doesn't come up?

Then we say so before anything else happens. Some damage sits under the clear coat; we show you on the test spot and either quote film or tell you it's a body shop's job.

Is the coating a warranty?

No. The years are its expected life with the care note followed. We check it once a year for free and top it up if it needs it.

What if you find something new?

We call before doing anything about it. Nothing goes on the bill that isn't on the agreement.

Do you take resprayed cars?

Yes, after a proper look. Resprays can have thin or soft clear coat, so we measure first and quote after inspection.

Can I wait while you work?

For washes and test spots, yes: the lounge has coffee and Wi-Fi. Correction, coating and film take days, so we drive you home if you live within five miles.

Not sure which?

Tell us about the car and what bothers you. We'll suggest the job, and a test spot if it needs one.
