How a job runs

  1. We look

    At the studio, under the lights, with you. For correction and coating that's a free 20-minute test spot.

  2. We agree

    What we'll do, what we'll leave, the price and how long you're without the car, in writing.

  3. We work

    If we find something new, we call before doing anything about it.

  4. You collect

    With a care note for the car, and payment by card or bank transfer when you're happy with it.
