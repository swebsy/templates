Where we work

Six areas of work, one sustained practice.

Coaching isn’t a menu. These are the questions that come up most often, in roughly the order they arrive.

i.

Executive presence

Lead from a clearer center. Move from competent to commanding without losing yourself in the process.

ii.

Career transitions

Make the leap with strategy and conviction — promotion, pivot, exit, founder path, or the next chapter altogether.

iii.

Decision-making under pressure

Untangle the hard call. Build the inner machinery to decide cleanly when stakes and ambiguity are both high.

iv.

Leading through change

Reorgs, layoffs, mergers, growth. Steady leadership when the ground under you keeps shifting.

v.

Difficult conversations

The board meeting. The co-founder split. The reorg announcement. Practice what you've been avoiding, before you're in it.

vi.

Sustaining the long game

Energy, attention, identity. Build a working life you can run for another twenty years without burning out what made you good.

How we work

Four principles we navigate by.

Coaching is the slow craft of attention. The convictions underneath every session — equal parts evidence-based practice and respect for what you’ve already built.

i

Slow is fast.

Real change moves at the pace of trust. Months, not minutes.

ii

Insight isn’t enough.

You already know most of what you need. The work is metabolizing it into action.

iii

The body keeps score.

Decisions and defenses live below the neck. We work with both head and body.

iv

No frameworks first.

We start with your situation. Frameworks come in only when they earn their keep.
