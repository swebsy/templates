# Craft — free portfolio website template

![Craft](thumb.webp)

A focused, minimal portfolio template for makers and creators who want their strongest projects and capabilities to take center stage.

Good for: Product designers, Independent makers, Creative developers.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/craft/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=craft&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/craft/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
