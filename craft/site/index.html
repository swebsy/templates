About

I’m a Senior Data Analyst with deep expertise in transforming raw, messy datasets into reliable, decision-ready intelligence. I take pride in building analytical frameworks that are rigorous, reproducible, and genuinely useful — bridging the gap between data engineering and the stakeholders who depend on accurate insight every day.

Currently, I'm on the Analytics Platform team at Stripe, where I own end-to-end reporting pipelines that serve product, finance, and operations across the company. I lead instrumentation design, dashboard strategy, and data quality initiatives, partnering with engineers and PMs to ensure every metric we ship is trustworthy and actionable.

Previously, I’ve worked across a range of industries — from high-growth fintech startups to enterprise software — including stints at Palantir, Twilio, and a Series B healthcare analytics company. Outside of my day job, I actively contribute to open-source data tooling and have published several guides on building robust test coverage for data pipelines. These experiences shaped my conviction that clean data and reliable systems are the foundation every good product decision rests on.
