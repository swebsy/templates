# Daybook Hair — free local business website template

![Daybook Hair](thumb.webp)

A salon home page laid out as the day book: one column per stylist, junior to director, each service block as tall as it takes, priced.

Good for: Hair salons, Barbers with several chairs, Salons that price by stylist level, Studios booking through Fresha.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/daybook-hair/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=daybook-hair&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/daybook-hair/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
