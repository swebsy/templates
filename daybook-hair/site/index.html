What “from” means.

Every price on the board is that stylist’s own, next to how long you’ll be in the chair. “From” is the price for hair to the shoulders. For colour, hair past the shoulder blades adds $25 and very thick hair adds $25. We tell you the number in a 15-minute consult, before any colour goes on. AUD, GST included.
