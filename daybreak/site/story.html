Our Story

One used roaster and a stubborn idea.

Daybreak opened on the corner of Maple & 2nd in 2016. Same corner, same roaster, same idea — coffee shouldn't be rushed.

We started with one used roaster and a stubborn idea — that coffee shouldn't be rushed.

Everything we pour begins as green beans we source ourselves and roast in tiny batches out back — often the same morning you drink it.

No syrupy shortcuts, no burnt espresso. Just careful roasting, honest prices, and a room that's glad to have you stay.
