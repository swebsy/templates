# Eighty Ten — free local business website template

![Eighty Ten](thumb.webp)

A massage studio and private bathhouse laid out as the walk through the building, each room its own colour at its own temperature.

Good for: Massage studios, Day spas and bathhouses, Saunas and cold plunge, Facial and skin studios.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/eighty-ten/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=eighty-ten&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/eighty-ten/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
