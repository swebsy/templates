Your first visit

Some people have never had a massage. These are the things they ask us at the front desk, answered before you book.

  • What do I wear?

    Underwear, or nothing if you prefer. You undress on your own, and we knock before we come back in.

  • How much of me is covered?

    Everything except the part we are working on. The towel moves with the massage.

  • Can I say “too hard”?

    Please do. We ask for a number from 1 to 5 at the start. Say a different number any time.

  • What does it cost in the end?

    The price on this page. Tea is included, and nobody sells you a cream at the door.

  • Will the sauna be full of strangers?

    No. The back rooms are booked privately, for one or two people at a time.

  • What if I need to cancel?

    It's free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge half.

Tea, then the door

Every visit ends on the bench in the lounge with a pot of tea. Stay as long as you like.
