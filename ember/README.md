# Ember — free local business website template

![Ember](thumb.webp)

A wood-fired restaurant site in warm paper and ember red: oversized serif display type, ruled editorial sections, priced menu lists and a booking-first call to action.

Good for: Restaurants and wine bars, Cafés and bakeries, Chef-led pop-ups, Any small room that takes bookings.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/ember/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=ember&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/ember/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
