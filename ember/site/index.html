Menu

Get to know us

Who are we

We are a small crew of cooks, growers and stubborn romantics who care more about the plate than the presentation. Elena opened the room in 2019 with one wood oven and a short list of suppliers she trusted. Very little has changed since — we still write the menu the morning it is served, and we still keep the last table free for whoever walks in.
