Allergies

Tell us when you book and again when you sit down. We cook over open fire in one room — we can adapt most dishes, but we cannot promise a trace-free kitchen.

Feed me

Five courses, whole table$85per person

The kitchen sends what it is proudest of that night: bread, two from the coals, a pasta, something large to share and one sweet. It is how we would feed you at home, and it is the best way to eat here on a first visit.

Wine pairing

Add five glasses chosen by Tom for $55 a head. Mostly Victorian, mostly low intervention, always poured with a reason.

How it works

Whole table only, and we need to know before you arrive. Tell us about allergies and we will build around them. Tables of seven or more eat this way as a rule.

Dietary

Most of the menu can be made gluten free, and there is a full vegetarian version of the feasting menu. Vegan takes a little more notice — tell us when you book.

Prices

All prices in AUD and include GST. A 10% surcharge applies on Sundays and public holidays. No surcharge for card.

Corkage
