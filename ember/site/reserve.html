Walk-ins

The bar and the two window seats are held for walk-ins every night. If the calendar looks full it is still worth trying your luck at 17:30.

Cancellations

Free up to 24 hours before. After that we charge $30 a head, because a table of four lost on a Saturday is a real hole in a forty-two seat room.

Large groups

Seven or more sit at the long table beside the fire. Call the room and ask for Marco — we will talk through a set menu and hold the whole end of the room for you. Groups of that size eat the feasting menu at $85 a head, which keeps the kitchen honest and the table talking rather than reading.

Deposit

$40 a head to hold the date, taken off the final bill. Refundable up to seven days out.

Final numbers

Confirmed 48 hours before. We buy for the table on the day, so that is the number we cook for.

One bill

Groups of seven or more settle on a single account. Sort it out among yourselves on the tram home.

Tell us first

There is a notes field on the booking form and we do read every one of them. These are the four that change what we do on the night.

Allergies

Tell us when you book and again when you sit down. We can adapt most of the menu, but we cook over open fire in one room and cannot promise a trace-free kitchen.

Vegetarian & vegan

There is a full vegetarian feasting menu at the same price. Vegan takes a day's notice and is worth asking for.

Occasions

Birthdays, anniversaries, the end of something. We will not sing, but there will be a candle and a glass of something on the house.

Access

Step-free from the Rathdowne Street entrance, including the bathrooms. Say so when you book and we will hold a table with room to move.

Before you ask

How far ahead can I book?

A month, to the day. The calendar opens at nine in the morning and Friday and Saturday tend to go the same week.

Can I request the fire table?

You can ask and we will try, but it is the table everyone wants and we cannot promise it. The bar seats have the better view of the grill anyway.

Do you take walk-ins?

Every night. The bar and two window seats are never booked out. Come at 17:30 for the best odds.

Is there a dress code?

No. Half the room comes straight from work and the other half has clearly thought about it, and both are right.

Can we bring our own wine?

Tuesday to Thursday, $25 a bottle, two bottles a table. If it is something special we will happily drink a glass with you.

What if we are only two and want to eat early?

The 17:30 sitting is the quietest, the easiest to book, and the only time you will hear each other properly. It is our favourite service of the week.
