One wedding a weekend: the other two days stay empty.

What the price covers, and what it doesn’t.

Included

  • The property from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m.
  • A rehearsal hour on Friday at 5, for Saturday weddings
  • 200 white ceremony chairs
  • Round tables for eight and 200 cross-back chairs
  • The chandeliers and the overhang furniture
  • One of us on site all day
  • Setup and cleanup of our furniture, and parking
  • The rain set-up: guests at their tables, the aisle and flowers moved inside, the bar opened indoors

Not included

  • Catering, from any licensed caterer
  • Bar service, by a licensed bartender
  • Linens and flowers
  • Music and an officiant
  • Rentals beyond our furniture

Required, and said up front

  • One-day event insurance, which you buy: about $150 to $200
  • A $750 damage deposit, returned after the weekend

What holds a date.

A signed contract and a $2,000 retainer hold your date. The retainer is part of the price and isn’t refundable. The balance is due 60 days before.

Come and walk it, rain or shine.

Tours take about 45 minutes, on weekday evenings and some Sundays. Send a request and Jo writes back with times within two working days.
