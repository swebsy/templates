Questions couples ask on the tour.

The rain question comes first because it comes first on every tour. Anything else, ask Jo: (740) 555-0148.

What happens if it rains?

If it rains

At 11 on the morning of, we read the hourly forecast with you, and if it’s close, you choose. If it’s the barn, your guests stay seated at their dinner tables, you walk an aisle across the open floor, and you say your vows under the chandeliers. The sound and the arch flowers move inside by 3 and the bar opens indoors, so nobody waits outside while a room is turned. All of it is in the price.

When do we get in?

The property is yours from 9 a.m. to 11 p.m., with music until 10:30. Saturday couples also get a rehearsal hour on Friday at 5.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes, any licensed caterer you like. We don’t keep a list you have to choose from.

Who runs the bar?

A licensed bartender you hire. You bring the drinks and they serve them.

How many guests fit?

Up to 200. Outdoors, 200 on white chairs at the gazebo. Indoors, 200 at round tables for eight with the floor left open, and for a rain ceremony the same 200 stay at their tables.

How far is the walk to the gazebo?

Paved all the way from the patio, about two minutes on foot. We drive anyone who’d rather not walk.

Is the barn heated and cooled?

Yes, it’s heated and air-conditioned, so an October evening and an August afternoon are both comfortable inside.

What decorations aren’t allowed?

Real candles in glass only. No confetti, glitter or rice, sparklers on the lawn only, and no fireworks.

Can our dog come?

Yes, to the ceremony, with someone to look after them.

Does a tour hold our date?

No. A signed contract and a $2,000 retainer hold your date; nothing else does.

Come and walk it, rain or shine.

Tours take about 45 minutes, on weekday evenings and some Sundays. Send a request and Jo writes back with times within two working days.
