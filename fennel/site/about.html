About Fennel

A practice built by people who hated going to the dentist.

Aoife opened Fennel in 2016 after fourteen years in practices that ran on fifteen-minute slots. The model here is the opposite.

Fewer patients a day · Longer appointments

The building

A converted print works on Colston Yard

Three surgeries, a lot of daylight, and no waiting room television. The chairs face a window rather than a ceiling poster, which sounds trivial until you have spent forty minutes in one.

How we work

Four things we will not trade away.

01

Thirty-minute minimum appointments. Nobody is rushed out of the chair to make a schedule work.

02

The same clinician every time, unless you ask otherwise. Continuity is most of good dentistry.

03

Written quotes before treatment, always including the option of doing nothing yet.

04

No commission on treatment. Nobody here earns more by recommending more.
