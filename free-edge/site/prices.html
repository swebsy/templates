Lengths

The length is the one you leave with, chosen when you book: your own nail grown out, shortened, or extended with a tip. It's measured on your longest finished nail and rounded to the nearest millimeter. We confirm it together before starting; if you change your mind in the chair, you hear the new total first.

Upkeep

Fills, removal and repair. Fills are offered every 3 to 4 weeks, with finishes and art added as above. After 4 weeks, or on another studio's work, it's a new set with removal. I use a soak-off structured gel; I don't remove acrylic, dip or hard gel.

Questions

Anything else: text (614) 555-0182.

Can I bring a screenshot?

Text it before you book. I'll price it with these parts, or tell you it isn't something I do.

Do you do acrylic?

No, structured gel only, and I don't remove acrylic or dip.

Pedicures?

No, hands only.

Walk-ins?

No, the suite door is locked between appointments.

My nails are already long.

Pick the length you want to leave with; if that's 8 to 12 mm past the fingertip, it's Long.

Can you fill another studio's set?

No. If it's gel or gel polish, I remove it (+$25 · +30 min) and do a new set; I don't remove acrylic, dip or hard gel.
