# Gather — free local business website template

![Gather](thumb.webp)

A glass studio that hand-blows lighting, dark by default: a lamp that fades up from dark, a wall of lights that switch on as you scroll, and a commission form.

Good for: Glassblowing and lighting studios, Ceramicists and furniture makers, Small makers who take commissions, Studios with open days or demos.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/gather/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=gather&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/gather/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
