Before you ask.

The five things people ask us most. Anything else, put it in the form.

Can I see a light before I order?

Yes. On Saturdays, 11 am to 4 pm, every light on this page hangs lit in the studio, and a light is blown on the hour. If Tacoma is too far, we post you a chip of the glass.

Will mine look like the photo?

Close, not identical: hand-blown glass shifts a little in colour and seed from one gather to the next. The chip we post comes from the batch your light is blown from.

Who wires it?

Eli wires and tests every light here with UL-listed parts. Pendants hang from a standard ceiling box; your electrician fits them in about twenty minutes.

Do you ship?

Anywhere in the US, double-boxed with the glass floated in foam. If a light arrives broken, send us a photo and we blow it again.

How does paying work?

Half when you approve the chip, by a link we email you; the rest before it ships. Nothing is paid on this site.
