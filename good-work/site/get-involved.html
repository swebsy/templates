Questions

Good to know before you give

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Good Work is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 88-1234567), and you'll get an emailed receipt for every gift.

Where does my money actually go?

Ninety-two cents of every dollar goes directly to programs. We publish an independently audited report every year — no mystery, no overhead bloat.

Can I cancel my monthly gift?

Anytime, in two clicks — no phone call and no guilt. Use the link in your receipt or just email us.

How do I start volunteering?

Pick a shift on this page or come to an orientation. No experience and no long-term commitment required — just show up.
