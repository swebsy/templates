Our mission

We back the people already doing the good work

Change rarely arrives from the outside. We find the neighbors, teachers, and organizers who show up every day — then give them the funding to do more of it.

Feed

Stocking community fridges and funding kitchens so no family in the neighborhood has to skip a meal.

Mentor

Pairing local kids with mentors and after-school programs that keep them learning, building, and belonging.

Rebuild

Fast emergency grants that help families recover from a fire, a flood, or a month that fell apart.

Maria's story

One grant kept a family in their home

When the plant closed, Maria fell two months behind on rent — three kids, no cushion. A neighbor nominated her for a Rebuild grant. Within 48 hours, Good Work covered the gap and connected her with a job-training partner.

“I didn't need a handout. I needed one month. Good Work gave me the room to catch my breath.”
Maria R.
Rebuild grant recipient, 2025
