Our story

The good work was already happening

Good Work started at a kitchen table in 2018, with one question: what if we funded the neighbors already showing up for each other, instead of building another org from scratch? Seven years later, that question still runs everything we do.

How we got here

The road so far

2018

A kitchen table and a spreadsheet

Five neighbors pooled $4,000 to cover a family's rent for a month. Word spread down the block, and then across it.

2020

Answering the emergency

When the pandemic hit, we moved $600,000 in direct relief in ten weeks — no red tape, no waiting lists.

2022

Programs, not one-offs

We turned scattered good deeds into standing programs — Feed, Mentor, and Rebuild — so the help keeps coming.

2024

Audited and accountable

We earned our first independent audit and a Platinum transparency rating — proof the numbers add up.

2025

3,100 volunteers strong

Today Good Work funds more than 90 neighborhood projects a year, powered by donors and volunteers who live nearby.
