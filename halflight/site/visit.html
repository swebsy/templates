How the door works.

Most of the room is never booked. Come when you like, with whoever you like.

Walk in
Fourteen stools at the bar and thirty seats in the room, first come.
When it's full
Leave a number at the door. We text you when there's room, so you can wait somewhere warmer.
Five to seven of you
Come before seven, or split between the bar and a table. We'll tell you honestly at the door.
Tables on Resy
A few room tables are on Resy. Tables for two to four, Tuesday to Thursday, on Resy ↗
Eight or more
The back booth seats 8 to 14. Plan a group night

Good to know.
