The questions people ask Maren on the first call.

How do you get in?

Most clients give us a key or a door code at the first visit, by phone. Never by email or the quote form.

Tell us their names. We keep doors the way you leave them, and it goes on the plan.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. If you'd like yours used, leave them out and we'll note it.

What don't you clean?

Inside the oven on regular visits, mold, outside windows, and anything that needs a ladder over six feet.

Give us 48 hours' notice and there's no fee.
