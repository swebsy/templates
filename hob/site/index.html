Nine weeks.
41 flats. 38 kept it.

Before the waitlist, 41 flats in Lisbon and Porto ran Hob for nine weeks in a private test. At the end, 38 were still using it, and most of them had stopped the dinner group chat.

“I used to text four people every afternoon. Now I tap once and know how much rice to make.”

Marta, a flat of four in Lisbon, private test

“The Sunday number ended a three-month argument about olive oil.”

Dan, a flat of three in Porto, private test

What Hob won't do.

  • Never asks for your bank.Hob keeps a tally. You pay each other however you already do.
  • No ads, now or later.We'd rather charge for extras than sell your dinners.
  • Free for flats up to six.Hob Plus (€2.99 a month per flat) comes later and never takes the free rota away.
  • iPhone first.Android flatmates answer from the link, like anyone without the app.

Questions.

Do my flatmates need the app?

No. One person runs the flat in the app; everyone else answers from a link in any browser.

What if only I use it?

Then it's still your rota and your list. Most flats in the test started with one person and the rest followed within a week.

What do you do with my email?

We use it once, to tell you testing is open, and then delete the list. Nothing else, no newsletter.

Who's behind Hob?

Inês Carvalho (design) and Tomás Reis (code), two flatmates in Porto who got tired of the group chat. Write to us at hello@hob.house.
