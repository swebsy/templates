Questions

You leave with every password.

Is there a contract?

The first 30 days, then month to month. Leave with 30 days' notice, no fee.

Two part-timers share a desk. Is that one desk?

Two. We count the people who use the computers, not the chairs. The plan says desk because most offices have one each.

We already have someone part-time. Can you work with them?

Yes. Many offices keep a person on site for small things and call us for the rest. We agree who does what in the one-page plan.

Do you come to the office?

Yes. Onsite visits in Rochester and the nearby towns are included in both plans; most happen the same or next working day.

Do we have to switch to Microsoft 365?

No. We support Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. If you're on something else, we'll tell you plainly whether it's worth moving.

What happens if we leave?

You get every admin password, a written map of your setup and an hour with your next IT provider, at no charge.
