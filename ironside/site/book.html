// Good To Know

Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Nope — walk-ins are always welcome. Booking just guarantees your slot and your barber, especially on weekends.

How should I pay?

Cash or card, whatever's easy. Tips are appreciated but never expected.

Can I request a specific barber?

Always. Pick your barber when you book online, or just ask at the desk when you walk in.

What about the kids?

Under-12 cuts are $28 and we keep it quick and calm. First-ever cut comes with a little keepsake.

Running late?

Give us a call. We hold your chair for ten minutes, then it goes to whoever's next up.
