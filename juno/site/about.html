about — the person behind the noise

I'm Juno Hale, and I make brands that refuse to whisper

Fifteen years of turning quiet briefs into things people actually remember. Brand systems, product design, and the occasional weird internet stunt.

the short version

I started in the messy middle of design and marketing, where nobody could agree whose job the logo was. Turns out that's exactly where the good work lives.

These days I work with founders and small teams who'd rather stand out than blend in. I build the whole system — identity, voice, product, the launch moment — so it all sounds like one confident thing instead of six timid ones.

Loud on purpose. Never loud by accident.
