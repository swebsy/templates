# Keane & Hurley — free services website template

![Keane & Hurley](thumb.webp)

An interior studio whose every photo is a room marked up with painter's tape, each numbered tab keyed to a priced list of what stayed and what was bought.

Good for: Interior designers, Home stylists, Decorators who price by the room.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/keane-hurley/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=keane-hurley&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/keane-hurley/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
