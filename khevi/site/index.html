Before you write

Questions.

When can we go?

Tusheti only from mid-June to mid-October, when the Abano road is open. Kakheti is best in September for the harvest; Kazbegi from June. Tbilisi and the wine country work all year.

Is the €450 refundable?

No, it pays for ten days of our work. It comes off the trip if you book through us, and the plan is yours either way.

Couldn't we just do it alone?

Tbilisi and Kakheti, yes, easily. Tusheti and Svaneti are where a driver who knows the road, and a guesthouse that expects you, matter.

Do you book flights?

No. We tell you which airport and which days work, and plan from the minute you land.

Can we travel with children?

Yes. We keep the longest day under four hours and say so on the plan.
