How a case works
  1. 1

    We file

    A complete petition, read twice before it goes. Most delays start with one missing page.

  2. 2

    USCIS confirms

    A receipt notice arrives two to four weeks later. From then on, we watch the case for you.

  3. 3

    Fingerprints

    A short visit to a local office, usually within two months of filing.

  4. 4

    The interview

    Often eight to fourteen months in. We run a mock interview with you first, in your language.

  5. 5

    Approved

    The card comes by mail. We check every line on it before you put it away.

Questions we hear on the first call.

If yours isn't here, ask us. The answer is free, even if you never hire us.

What does the flat fee cover?

Everything we do on your case, from the first form to the interview, in one price you see in writing before we start. Government fees are separate, and we list those too.

Do I need a lawyer at all?

Not always. If your case is simple, we'll say so on the call and point you to the forms. Most people who hire us have a past visa, an old denial or a deadline.

Can you guarantee my approval?

No one honestly can. We promise a complete file, read twice before it goes, and that you will always know where your case stands.

Can we meet by video?

Yes. About half our clients never visit the office. We meet by video, sign by email, and mail you the originals to keep.

Do you take cases outside California?

Immigration is federal law, so yes. We represent families in every state, and we prepare you for an interview at any USCIS office.

My case was denied before. Can you help?

Bring the denial letter to the consultation. Kwame will read it with you and tell you whether a new filing, an appeal or a waiver makes sense.
