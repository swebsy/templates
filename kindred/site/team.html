Two partners, one office, four languages.

Marisol and Kwame take every case themselves. Two paralegals and an office manager keep the files moving, and someone always answers in your language.

Marisol Solano

Partner. English, español

Marisol came to Sacramento from Michoacán at nine. She spent her teens reading USCIS letters aloud at the kitchen table, and her twenties as a DACA recipient who could not count on the next two years.

She studied law at UC Davis, opened this practice with Kwame in 2013, and still takes the first call on most Spanish-language cases.

Admitted in California, 2011. Member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association.

Kwame Mensah

Partner. English, Twi, français

Kwame's parents came to Columbus, Ohio, from Kumasi in 1984. He spent six years as a USCIS officer in Sacramento, deciding marriage and family petitions.

He left to sit on the other side of the desk. He reads every file the way an officer will, and prepares every client for the interview himself.

Admitted in Ohio, 2006, and California, 2013. Member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association.
