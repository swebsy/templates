How an episode is made

Morven · 00:00 · door locked

The owner turns the sign to Closed and locks the door. I set the recorder on the counter, beside the till.

[sign turned to closed]
[keys on the hook by the door]
Morven · 00:03 · kettle on

Nobody talks properly until there's a cup in their hand, so the first few minutes never make the episode.

Morven · 00:08 · recording

Always the same first question: tell me what you sell. Then we go wherever the shop takes us.

Morven · 00:51 · at the till

I ask what I should buy, and I pay full price for it. The item and the price close the episode.

[the till, drawer shut]

Gear: one handheld Zoom recorder and two clip mics. No studio, and no headphones on the guest.

Be a guest

Morven · to shopkeepers

Somebody told you that you should be on this. Here's what I look for.

  • You own the shop and you run it.
  • You've been trading five years or more.
  • You're within an hour of Kirkcaldy.

You don't have to be funny, and I won't make you sound daft: you hear the edit before it goes out. I reply to every offer within two weeks.
