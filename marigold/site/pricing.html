The questions people actually ask.

Usually on the phone, usually in this order.

What happens after the 30 days?

We email you twice beforehand and then stop. Nothing charges automatically, and your data waits ninety days in case you change your mind.

Do you take a cut of my payments?

No. Card fees go to Stripe at their published rate and land in your account. Marigold adds nothing on top.

Can I move my existing diary across?

Yes, and we do it. Send whatever you have — a CSV, a competitor export, or photographs of a paper book — and we will match it appointment for appointment.

What counts as a location?

An address with its own diary. A second chair in the same room is a staff member, not a location, and staff are included.

Does it work offline?

The day view keeps working when the broadband drops, and everything you do syncs when it returns. Payments need a connection.

More than five locations?

Then the pricing is a conversation rather than a table. It stays per location, and it gets cheaper per site.

Already paying for something worse?

We will cover the rest of your current contract, up to six months, once you have been with us for sixty days. Send the invoice; there is no form.
