The product

Everything the front desk does, in one place.

Four jobs, built to be finished rather than configured. No modules to buy, no setup consultant, no per-seat maths.

Every plan includes all four. The price changes with how many locations you run, never with which features you are allowed to touch.

Scheduling

The calendar everyone can read.

Chairs, rooms, technicians and vans across one grid. Drag to move an appointment and the client is told before you have let go of the mouse.

  • Colour-blocked by staff member, not by a legend you have to learn
  • Double-booking is refused at the point of booking, not flagged after
  • Holiday, sick days and split shifts come out of the same grid
  • Works on the tablet by the door and the phone in your pocket
Client records

The regular's history, not the receptionist's memory.

Colour formulas, allergies, the fact that she hates the radio on. It lives on the record, so a new starter treats her like a regular on day one.

Records are exportable in full, at any time, in a format a human can read. If you leave, you leave with everything.

Payments

Money that settles itself.

Take a deposit when the appointment is made, the balance when it is done, and let Marigold do the chasing in between.

Tips split by your own rules at close. Takings reconcile against the till without anyone opening a spreadsheet.

Messaging

Messages people actually answer.

Sent in the window where somebody will still move an appointment rather than quietly not turn up. Reply in words; there is no app to install and no link to tap.

Written in your voice, not ours. Edit every template, or leave the defaults — they were written by people who have worked a front desk.
