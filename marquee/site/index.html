What it is

A room wherenobody issomewhere else.

Most conferences ask you to choose. Six rooms, four tracks, and the talk everyone is quoting afterwards is the one you missed. Marquee runs one stage, from the first coffee to the last question, so that for two days everyone in the building has seen exactly the same thing. The hallway conversation gets much better when it has something to be about.

No tracks

One stage, one running order. You will not miss anything, because there is nothing to miss.

Thirty minutes

Every talk is the same length. Long enough for an argument, short enough to keep it honest.

Recorded, freely

Every talk goes online, free, within a month. The ticket buys you the room, not the video.

Questions

Things people ask before they buy

If yours isn't here, write to us. A person reads that inbox and usually answers the same day.

hello@marquee.example

Will the talks be streamed?

No. We tried it in 2025 and it made the room worse — speakers played to the camera instead of the seats. Every talk is recorded and published free within a month, and the recordings are genuinely good. The live thing is for the people who came.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Yes, to anyone, any time up to the morning of day one. Send us the new name. There's no fee and we won't ask why.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund until 1 April, no questions. After that we'll refund if we can resell the seat, which we usually can. If something has gone wrong in your life, ask us anyway.

Is the venue accessible?

Step-free from the street to the stalls, the bar and the accessible toilets. Twelve wheelchair spaces with companion seating, a hearing loop across the whole stalls, and live captioning on the main screen for every talk. The balcony is stairs only.

Is there food?

Lunch both days is included and served in the circle bar — hot, sat down, not a sandwich in a box. Tell us about allergies and diets at checkout and we will actually cater for them.

I've never been to one of these. Will I know anyone?

Probably not, and that is the normal case. There's a first-timers' coffee at 08:45 on day one in the upstairs bar, hosted by people whose job that morning is to introduce you to somebody.
