Running order09:00 — 18:00Both days · Ambassador Theatre

Nothing runsat the sametime as anything.

Every talk is thirty minutes with ten for questions. Breaks are long on purpose — the corridor is half the point. If a talk overruns, the break absorbs it, not the next speaker.

Times are fixed and published early so you can book flights around them. The running order may shift; the clock will not.
