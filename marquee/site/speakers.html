Half the programme comes from people we have never heard of

Propose a talk

You do not need a track record, a following, or a title. You need one thing you have actually done and an argument about it. Proposals are read blind — we strip the names before the committee sees them.

What to send

A title, two hundred words, and one sentence on why you are the person to say it.

What you get

Travel, two nights at the Ashbury, a fee of $1,200, and four rehearsal sessions with a coach if you want them.

When you hear

Everyone gets an answer by 20 January, including the four hundred we cannot fit.

Nine days is how long it took last year

Take a seatwhile therestill is one.
