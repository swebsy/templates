Before you buy

The small print, in plain words

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — three payments, no interest, no credit check, chosen at checkout. Your seat is held from the first payment.

Do I need to pick my seat?

No. Seating is unreserved and the room is small enough that there is no bad one. Accessible seating is the exception and is booked in advance.

Is a workshop pass worth it?

If you want to make something with your hands for half a day alongside twenty other people, yes. If you mostly want the talks, no — buy the standard ticket and spend the difference on the hotel.

What if my visa is refused?

Full refund on sight of the refusal, at any point, including the week before. We will also write you an invitation letter within two working days of asking.

Will there be a waiting list?

Yes, and it moves — about forty tickets come back each year. Join it even if it looks long.
