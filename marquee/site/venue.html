The roomAmbassador Theatre2110 Woodward Avenue · Detroit, MI

Built in 1927for vaudeville.Still perfect.

Nine hundred seats, a stage you can see from every one of them, and acoustics designed before anyone had a microphone. It was a cinema for forty years and nearly a car park for two. We are very glad it isn't.

Doors on Woodward. Registration is in the lobby, and it takes about ninety seconds.

Getting here

It is on the main road, which helps

By air

Detroit Metropolitan (DTW) is 40 minutes by cab, about $55. The Michigan Flyer coach runs hourly to downtown for $18.

By rail

Amtrak arrives at New Center, ten minutes down Woodward on the QLine. Chicago is five and a half hours, Toronto four.

On the QLine

Grand Circus Park stop is ninety seconds' walk. Trams every fifteen minutes, $1.50, and free with a conference lanyard.

Driving

The Broderick garage on Witherell is a two-minute walk, $12 a day. Do not park on Woodward itself — they will tow you and they are quick about it.

What the building can and cannot do

It is a hundred years old, so some of this is a compromise. Tell us what you need and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it.

access@marquee.example
Step-free

Street to lobby, stalls, bar and accessible toilets, with a lift to the circle. The balcony is stairs only and always will be.

Wheelchair spaces

Twelve, in the stalls, each with a companion seat next to it. Booked in advance and never released to general sale.

Captions and loop

Live captioning on the main screen for every talk, and a hearing loop across the whole stalls. ASL interpretation on request by 1 April.

Quiet room

Rehearsal room C, open all day, both days. No screens, no talking, a door that closes. Nobody will ask why you are in there.

Lighting and sound

No strobes at any point. Speakers are told, and we check the slides. Peak sound sits around 85 dB and ear plugs are free at the desk.

While you are here

Stay the weekend. It is worth it.
