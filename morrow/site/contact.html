Before you write

The questions we’re usually asked first.

Do you take small projects?

We occasionally design a focused courtyard or entry garden, but most work considers the whole residential property.

Can you build another designer’s plan?

Sometimes. We first review the drawings for buildability, planting intent, drainage, and enough detail to price responsibly.

What does a project cost?

After the site consultation, we provide a realistic design fee and construction range before asking you to proceed.
