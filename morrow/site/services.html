Services / Design to stewardship

One studio. Every season.

Landscape architects, builders, and horticulturists working as one team—from the first contour line to the garden’s fifth spring.

01Integrated service

Landscape design

A clear, buildable idea for the whole property.

We combine architecture, grading, planting, water, materials, and maintenance into one coherent plan. Every decision is drawn against real costs and real construction methods.

Typical scope

  • Site analysis + measured base plan
  • Concept design + rendered views
  • Materials, planting + lighting plans
  • Construction documentation
02Integrated service

Garden construction

The same thinking, carried carefully into the ground.

Our field team builds the work we design. That continuity protects the small alignments—the stone edge, the soil depth, the framed view—that make a landscape feel resolved.

Typical scope

  • Demolition, grading + drainage
  • Stone, concrete + carpentry
  • Irrigation + low-voltage lighting
  • Soil, planting + establishment
03Integrated service

Seasonal stewardship

Care that protects the original idea as it grows.

New gardens change quickly. We return at the moments that matter to tune structure, guide succession, and help clients understand the living system around them.

Typical scope

  • First-year establishment visits
  • Seasonal pruning + editing
  • Soil and irrigation calibration
  • Long-term garden direction
