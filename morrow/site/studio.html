Studio / Vancouver, BC

Built like architecture. Tended like habitat.

Morrow is a small landscape studio where design, construction, and horticulture share the same table.

How Morrow began

The gap was always between the drawing and the dirt.

Morrow began in 2013 after too many good landscape plans lost their intent between designer, estimator, builder, and maintenance crew.

So we built a different kind of practice. Designers price with builders. Builders review drawings before they leave the studio. Horticulturists shape the planting from first palette through establishment. The result is less translation—and landscapes that keep the intelligence of the first idea.

“The garden is not the soft part around the architecture. It is the architecture that changes.”
