How it stays honest

Every unit is calibrated against a reference instrument before it leaves the line, and the certificate number is printed inside the battery-free base.

The carbon dioxide sensor then keeps itself honest with automatic baseline correction: it assumes that, over any seven-day window, the lowest reading it saw was fresh air at 420 ppm, and re-zeroes to that. If the device lives somewhere that never gets fresh air — a grow tent, a server room, a boat — turn that off in settings and calibrate manually instead.

  • Factory calibration5 point · against Vaisala GMP252
  • Baseline correction window7 days · defeatable
  • Expected drift< 2% per year
