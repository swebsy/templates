Quick fixes

The six things support is asked about most, in the order they come up.

  • The display is blank but the status light is on

    The e-ink refresh has stalled. Hold the button on the base for five seconds and the face redraws. If it is still blank after that, the panel has failed and the repair is covered.

  • Readings look far too high in the first few days

    Give it a week. Both the volatile-organics index and the carbon dioxide baseline need roughly seven days of ordinary use before the numbers mean anything.

  • It will not join my Wi-Fi

    Nimbus One is 2.4 GHz only. If your router publishes one network name across both bands, split them temporarily during setup, or join it to the guest network and move it afterwards.

  • Carbon dioxide reads 400 ppm and never moves

    Automatic baseline correction has re-zeroed against a wrong low. Put the device outside for twenty minutes, then run Settings → Sensors → Calibrate manually. If the room never gets fresh air, turn automatic correction off entirely.

  • The app cannot find the device, but the device says it is online

    Your phone and the monitor are on different networks — usually a guest SSID or a separate IoT VLAN. Discovery uses mDNS, which most guest networks deliberately block.

  • Where do I find the serial number?

    Moulded into the base, just below the wall-mount keyhole, prefixed NB1‑. You will need it for a warranty claim, and support will ask for it first.

Firmware release notes

Updates install overnight by default. Every release since launch is listed here, including the ones that only fixed something — and including what we know is still broken.

  • 2.4.114 Jul 2026Current release
    FixedAutomatic baseline correction could re-zero carbon dioxide against a false low recorded during a power cut, leaving the reading stuck near 400 ppm.
    FixedMatter controllers occasionally saw humidity as unavailable after a Wi-Fi reconnect.
    ChangedThe status light now stays off between 22:00 and 07:00 unless a threshold is crossed.
  • 2.4.02 Jun 2026
    AddedThe local endpoint now reports raw particle counts per size bin alongside the mass estimate.
    AddedThe display can be set to show carbon dioxide as the primary figure instead of particulates.
    ChangedWarm-up from cold shortened from 90 seconds to 60.
  • 2.3.228 Apr 2026
    FixedDevices on networks running WPA3 transition mode failed to reconnect after a router restart and had to be re-paired.
  • 2.3.011 Mar 2026
    AddedMatter 1.3 certification. Devices already in the field gain Matter support without re-pairing.
    KnownHome Assistant discovery needs the device restarting once after this update. Still true; a fix is in testing.
