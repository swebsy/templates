Spotting a fake

Copies using our packaging have turned up on marketplaces since March. A genuine unit has its serial moulded into the base — raised plastic you can feel — beginning NB1‑, and it registers in the app. A printed sticker is the tell.

If yours will not register, send us the serial and where you bought it. We will tell you what you have, and if it is a fake we will help you get your money back whether or not you bought it from us.
