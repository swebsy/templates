Before you book

How many nights is the minimum?

+

Two nights, and three over Christmas, New Year and Easter week.

What if we need to cancel?

+

Cancel up to 14 days before arrival for a full refund. After that we keep the first night, unless we can give the room to someone else.

Can we bring children?

+

Children 12 and over are welcome. The terraces sit on the cliff edge, so the house isn't safe for younger ones.

When can we see whales?

+

Gray whales pass from December to April, most often in the morning. The Square and the Ledge see the most of them.

What does the price include?

+

The room for two and breakfast. Dinner at the long table is $65 a person, Thursday to Sunday. Prices add 16% IVA and 4% state lodging tax.
