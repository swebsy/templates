# Ottoline — free local business website template

![Ottoline](thumb.webp)

A neighbourhood florist where every stem hangs on a kraft tag with its price, its months and where it grew, plus same-day delivery, weddings by month and an order form.

Good for: Florists, Flower shops, Local makers with a price list.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/ottoline/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=ottoline&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/ottoline/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
