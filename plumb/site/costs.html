What the price covers, and what it doesn’t

Included

  • Design drawings and material choices with Ana
  • Permits and every city inspection
  • Materials, labor and our licensed electrician and plumber
  • Dust walls, floor protection and a daily sweep
  • Haul-away and a final clean
  • A two-year warranty on our work

Not included

  • Appliances (we’ll install the ones you buy)
  • Structural work an engineer has to sign off
  • Asbestos removal, done by a licensed abatement firm
  • Furniture, window treatments and decor
  • Anything you add after the contract is signed

If we find something behind the walls, we stop, show you and price it before we do it. Nothing changes without your yes.

From first call to handover

Six steps. You’ll always know which one you’re in and what comes next.

  1. 1

    A site visit

    An hour at your house with Nils or Ana, free, usually within a week of your call.

  2. 2

    A written estimate

    Line by line, within a week of the visit, with what’s included and what isn’t.

  3. 3

    Design and permits

    Three to six weeks of drawings, samples and the city’s sign-off. You sign one fixed price at the end of it.

  4. 4

    The build

    The weeks in the ledger above. Ana sends a short update every Friday, with photos.

  5. 5

    Handover

    On the last day we walk the room with you and leave a folder: warranties, paint codes, permits and the receipts.

  6. 6

    A check-in a year later

    We come back after a full Minnesota winter and fix anything that’s moved.

Questions people ask

Something else? Call Ana on (612) 555-0148.

Do you take small jobs?

Our smallest is a bathroom, around $28,000. For repairs and odd jobs we’ll point you to a handyman we trust.

How much do you take up front?

Ten percent when you sign, then payments tied to finished stages. The last 10% is due after the walkthrough.

Can we live in the house during the work?

Almost always. We seal off the room, keep one bathroom working and clean up every day.

How far ahead are you booked?

Usually three to four months for the build. Site visits happen within a week, so call early.

Planning work on an old house?

Ana answers every inquiry within two working days, and the first visit is free.
