How a job starts

Three steps, and you know the price before anyone picks up a hammer.

  1. 1

    We come out for an hour

    Free, with Nils or Ana. We look at the whole house, not just the room: the wiring, the joists, what’s behind the plaster.

  2. 2

    A written estimate within a week

    Line by line, with what’s included and what isn’t. Most people have it in four or five days.

  3. 3

    One fixed price, signed before we start

    The step that sets your price. If we find something behind the walls later, we show you and price it before we touch it.
