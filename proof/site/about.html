Pre-order

Two bakers and a counter on Central Avenue.

Ostrem Bakery opened in 2019 in a former shoe repair shop at 2210 Central. We still bake everything in the room behind the counter, and you can watch from the window.

Hours and directions

Anders Ostrem

Bread · since 2019

Anders learned to bake from his grandmother in Duluth and spent six years on the bread team at a hotel downtown. He mixes every dough himself and is usually at the oven by four.

Leila Amari

Pastry · since 2021

Leila ran the pastry kitchen at a restaurant in St. Paul before she joined. She laminates the croissant dough by hand every afternoon and makes every cake to order.

How we got here.

2019

Anders opens with one deck oven, a country loaf and a baguette. Sold out by ten most days.

2021

Leila joins as a partner. Croissants, tarts and cakes come with her.

2023

A second oven, and a bread for each day of the week. The Saturday limpa starts a line.

Today

Four cafés, five mornings a week, and Sam at the counter keeping a limpa back for the regulars.

Where the flour and butter come from

Flour

Stone-milled wheat, rye and spelt from a small mill in Northfield, an hour south.

Butter

Cultured butter from a dairy co-op in southern Minnesota, for every croissant and cake.

Fruit

Whatever is good at the Saturday market. That’s why the tarts change every week.
