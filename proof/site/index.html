Start a pre-order

Thirty-six hours from flour to shelf.

Mixed at 7pm, cold for a night and a day, on the shelf by 7am two days later. It is slow on purpose.

7pm, day one: mix

Flour from a Minnesota mill, water and our levain, folded by hand, then into the cold room for the night and the next day.

4am, day three: shape and score

Each loaf is shaped by hand and cut with a razor so it opens in the oven. The long cold is where the flavor comes from.

5am to noon: bake

On stone in small batches, the first out by 7am and the last just before noon, so there is fresh bread at lunch too.

Bread by Anders.
Pastry by Leila.

Anders opened the bakery in 2019 with one deck oven and a country loaf. Leila joined in 2021 and brought croissants, tarts and the cakes people now plan birthdays around.
