Questions people actually ask

What counts as an agent run?

One issue handed to an agent, from the first token to the pull request. Retries on a failed run are free — you are not billed for our bad day.

Do you charge for inactive members?

No. Seats are billed on the members who opened Pulsar that month, prorated automatically.

Can we self-host?

On Enterprise, yes — in your own cloud account, with the same release cadence as our hosted build.

What happens when we outgrow Free?

Nothing locks. You get a notice at ten members and a month to decide, and your data stays readable and exportable either way.
