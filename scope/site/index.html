Before you ask

The useful questions.

The ones that determine whether the sprint works—not the ones written for search engines.

Can ten days really be enough?

Yes, when the scope and feedback rhythm are protected. Larger sites are split into a focused first release and clearly priced follow-on work.

Do we need finished copy?

No. Bring your product knowledge and rough material. Positioning, page structure, and launch-ready copy are part of the sprint.

What happens after launch?

You receive the site, source assets, and a practical handoff. Continue independently, book a follow-on sprint, or move into the Design Desk.

Is everything really ours?

Yes. Approved work transfers to you at final payment. No proprietary theme license and no required Scope retainer.
