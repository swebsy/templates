Practical details

Before we begin.

How many people will work on our project?

A two-person senior team leads strategy, design, and build. Specialists join only when the scope genuinely calls for them.

What counts as one Design Desk request?

A focused deliverable such as a landing page, pitch deck, campaign concept, or defined group of product screens. Larger requests are divided into clear stages.

Can the sprint include a new logo?

A complete identity can be added before the website sprint. Lighter visual refreshes are often covered inside the standard engagement.

What is not included?

Complex application development, ecommerce operations, long-form content production, and ongoing campaign management are scoped separately.
