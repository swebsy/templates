THE USEFUL BRIEF

Five things make a great first email.

No polished deck required. A few honest sentences are enough.

Write your brief

What are you launching or changing?

The product, service, audience, and current state.

Why now?

The deadline, opportunity, or friction creating urgency.

What must be included?

Pages, deliverables, integrations, and non-negotiables.

What is the target date?

A real launch date or the window you are aiming for.

The person or group who can keep reviews moving.
