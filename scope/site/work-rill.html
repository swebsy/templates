Rill · Brand launch · 2026

A botanical drink with shelf presence.

Positioning, identity, packaging, campaign system, and launch site for a sparkling tea that refused to whisper.

The assignment

Make ritual feel refreshing.

Rill had a distinctive product and an indistinct category problem: botanical drinks either looked medicinal or disappeared into wellness beige.

The founders needed a position that felt credible to specialty retailers without sanding away the energy that made early customers care.

We centered the brand on a simple tension—grown slowly, enjoyed immediately—and built a visual system that paired natural materials with assertive commercial color.

Design language

RILL

A compact wordmark, an expanding leaf-line device, and color that holds its own from a fridge door.

Warm ivory · Botanical cobalt · Citrus orange · Ink

The system

Natural materials. Commercial confidence.

Position

A better afternoon ritual

Not another health claim. A bright, grown-up alternative to the second coffee and the first cocktail.

Identity

Rhythm you can recognize

Repeating botanical lines, hard crops, and compact typography gave every format the same energetic cadence.

Launch

One system, every surface

Packaging, retail sell-in, social launch, sampling materials, and the website all moved as one campaign.
