Repair or replace?

We’ll tell you to repair until it stops making sense: usually when the system is past 12 to 15 years in salt air and the repair is more than a third of a new one.

Most 2.5 to 4 ton heat pumps run $8,400 to $12,500 installed, with the permit. The quote visit is free and waits behind the day’s repairs.

Heat pumps and the cold nights

Most systems here are heat pumps, and we fix both directions. In office hours, below 60° inside on a cold night counts like 90° in August.

What we don’t do: gas furnaces, ductwork redesign, commercial systems over 10 tons, pool heaters.
