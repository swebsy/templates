When we come

We aim for the cooler months because a tune-up then is least likely to take a hot day’s slot. Once booked, your window holds like any other. A visit we can’t fit carries into the next month; visits don’t expire inside the plan year.

March or April

Before the heat, so the system starts the summer clean.

October or November

After it, to catch what the summer wore down.

Ten things we do each visit

  1. 1Rinse the outdoor coil
  2. 2Check the indoor coil and drain pan
  3. 3Flush and treat the drain line
  4. 4Measure refrigerant pressures and the temperature split
  5. 5Tighten the electrical connections
  6. 6Test the capacitor and contactor
  7. 7Check the blower
  8. 8Replace a standard 1-inch filter
  9. 9Check the thermostat
  10. 10Leave a written note of what we found
