Lan answers. Hải steers. Thảo comes with you.

Slackwater is one boat and one family, from an office at 27 Hạ Long Street in Bãi Cháy, since 2016.

  • Lan

    Bookings · ashore, 7:00–20:00

    She stays ashore all day so someone always answers. Send her your hotel and any food you don't eat.

  • Hải

    Skipper · by boat

    He grew up on these islands and has steered our boat since 2016. He waits aboard with tea for anyone skipping a cave.

  • Thảo

    Guide · by kayak and on foot

    First-aid trained. She paddles her own kayak at Luồn and climbs Ti Top's steps with you.

  • Bà Hoa

    Cook · lunch

    Hải's mother. Lunch is what she cooks at home: six dishes, seafood or vegetarian.

Why twelve

Twelve guests is up to six kayaks, which one guide can keep in sight at Luồn, and a group small enough to move as one.
