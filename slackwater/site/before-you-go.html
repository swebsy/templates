What each way asks of you.

By kayak

About 40 minutes, flat and sheltered.

No experience needed; your jacket stays on the whole time and Thảo shows you the stroke on the pontoon. Phones in a pocket that closes.

Rather not paddle? The rowed bamboo boat, when the station is running it.

On foot

About 10 minutes up; about 400 steps.

Steps up to Sửng Sốt, then uneven lit paths inside. At Ti Top, about 400 steps up to the lookout and back down the same staircase. Wear shoes with grip.

Rather not? Stay aboard with Hải at Sửng Sốt, about an hour; stay on Ti Top's beach, where Thảo meets you.

By boat

About 4 hr 15 min, in four legs.

Two covered decks with windows and an open deck on top; a toilet below. Lunch on board, water, tea and fruit all day.

Bring

  • Shoes with grip (sandals with a back strap work)
  • Quick-dry clothes and swimwear
  • A hat and sunscreen
  • Cash for drinks on board

Weather and season

We sail in any weather the authority allows; the caves are dry and there are ponchos on board. If boats can't sail, you get your money back or another day.

Storm season is roughly June to October; March to May and October to November are usually calmest.
