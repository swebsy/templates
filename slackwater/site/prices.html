When something can't run

Quảng Ninh's maritime authority decides when boats sail, and can call them back during the day.

Before the day

  • You cancel: free up to 24 hours before pickup; after that, no refund. The booking page shows this before you pay.
  • Boats can't sail, or we cancel for any reason: a full refund or another day, your choice.

On the day

  • Rain: we sail in any weather the authority allows; the caves are dry and there are ponchos on board.
  • Stops can change order to suit the harbor's traffic; times move with it.
  • The kayak point is closed: we go through by bamboo boat if the station runs it. If neither runs, we refund the stop and give the time to Ti Top's beach.
  • Kayaks run but the bamboo boat doesn't: children under 5, their parent and anyone who can't paddle wait aboard with Hải, about 45 minutes with tea and fruit. A paying guest who waits gets the Luồn refund.
  • Sửng Sốt or Ti Top is closed: we refund the stop and spend the time on board or at the other stop.

Questions

Can I come alone?

Yes. You'll share a kayak with another guest, or take the bamboo boat when the station runs it.

Is it hard?

About 40 minutes of flat-water paddling and some optional steps.

What each way asks, on Before you go

What if I can't manage the steps?

Skip Sửng Sốt and stay aboard with Hải. At Ti Top, stay on the beach and Thảo meets you there.

I've never kayaked.

You step in from a floating pontoon while the station's staff hold the kayak, Thảo shows you the stroke, and if you tire she tows you back.

Vegetarian lunch?

Yes; say so when you book.

Do you pick up from Hanoi?

No. We pick up in Bãi Cháy, or meet us at Tuần Châu harbor.
