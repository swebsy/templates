  1. 7:30

    Pickup

    The minibus at your hotel in Bãi Cháy. Staying elsewhere? Meet us at the main door of Tuần Châu harbor's ticket hall at 8:00.

  2. 8:00

    On foot

    Tuần Châu harbor

    Tickets and life jackets at the ticket hall, then on board.

  3. 8:15

    By boat

    Out into the bay

    An hour among the islands, with tea on deck.

  4. 9:15

    On foot

    Sửng Sốt cave

    About 10 minutes of steps up.

    Then about 45 minutes inside on lit, uneven paths, and 5 back down.

    Rather not? Stay aboard with Hải, with tea and fruit.

  5. 10:15

    By boat

    To Luồn cave

    A short hop.

  6. 10:30

    By kayak

    Luồn cave

    About 40 minutes of paddling.

    Two to a kayak through the cave into the lagoon and back, with Thảo in her own kayak. You step in from the station's pontoon.

    Rather not paddle? The rowed bamboo boat, when the station is running it.

  7. 11:15

    By boat

    Lunch on board

    Six dishes from Bà Hoa, seafood or vegetarian, on the way to Ti Top.

  8. 12:45

    On foot

    Ti Top island

    About 400 steps up.

    To the lookout and back down the same staircase, then the beach for a swim. It's the busiest stop on the route; the view is why.

    Rather stay on the beach? Thảo meets you there.

  9. 14:15

    By boat

    Back to the harbor

    On deck, or a nap below.

  10. 15:45

    Tuần Châu harbor

    The minibus has you at your hotel by 16:15.

If something can't run

Stops can change order on the day to suit the harbor's traffic. If the kayak point is closed, we go by bamboo boat when the station runs it; if neither runs, or another stop is closed, we refund that stop's share. The amounts, for shared and private trips, are on Prices.
