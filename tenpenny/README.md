# Tenpenny — free local business website template

![Tenpenny](thumb.webp)

A tattoo studio page told as a stencil coming into register: off in the hero, nearer each section, and in place at the enquiry form.

Good for: Tattoo studios, Piercing and body art studios, Custom illustration studios.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/tenpenny/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=tenpenny&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/tenpenny/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
