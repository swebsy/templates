# Undercoat — free local business website template

![Undercoat](thumb.webp)

A dog groomer's site built around each coat's routine: how often it comes back, the finish, prices by coat and a short new-dog enquiry.

Good for: Independent dog groomers, One- and two-table grooming salons, Groomers who book through MoeGo or similar, Mobile and home-based groomers.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/undercoat/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=undercoat&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/undercoat/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
