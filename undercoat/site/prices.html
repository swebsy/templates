What moves a price.

We tell you at drop-off if we expect any of these, and nothing changes without your yes.

A matted coat

Brushing at $2 a minute, 15 minutes at most. Past that, we call you.

Longer than asked

A coat well past its finish takes longer to bring back.

A dog who needs breaks

We go slower and stop when they've had enough.
