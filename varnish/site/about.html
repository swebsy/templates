about the painter

Mara Voss is a paintings conservator specializing in the careful treatment of celebrated works from museum, chapel, and private collections. Trained in traditional materials and modern conservation science, her practice centers on close looking, patient testing, and respect for the artist's hand. Each project begins with documentation and restraint, then moves toward treatments that stabilize fragile surfaces, recover tonal clarity, and preserve the history held in every layer.

Voss has contributed to the restoration of landmark oil paintings, devotional panels, and large civic commissions, working alongside curators, registrars, and collection stewards. Her treatments include varnish reduction, structural repair, retouching, and preventive guidance for works that continue to travel, exhibit, and teach.

Today, her studio focuses on complex restorations where public recognition and material vulnerability meet. Recent projects examine how age, light, previous repairs, and environmental change alter the reading of a famous image over time. Voss approaches each painting as both an object and a record, balancing technical intervention with the quiet discipline of knowing when to leave evidence undisturbed.
