# Wren & Wildflower — free link in bio website template

![Wren & Wildflower](thumb.webp)

A warm editorial link-in-bio template for small shops and makers, with tactile link buttons, featured products, and a soft clay and sage palette.

Good for: Independent makers, Small product brands, Boutique shops.

- [Live demo](https://studio.swebsy.com/templates/wren-wildflower/preview/)
- [Edit in Swebsy](https://studio.swebsy.com/create?template=wren-wildflower&ref=github), the free visual editor, no account needed
- [Template details](https://swebsy.com/templates/wren-wildflower/)

## Use it

`site/` is the whole website. Open `site/index.html` locally, or upload the
folder to any static host. Replace `site/robots.txt` rules as needed and add a
sitemap once you know your domain (Swebsy writes one when you set a Site URL).
